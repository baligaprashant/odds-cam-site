Odds Cam

Odds Cam is a poker odds calculator for learning and practice. Point your iPhone camera at playing cards, or pick them by hand, and see each hand's win and tie chances. It is a learning tool only: it does not take bets or handle money, and it must not be used for gambling of any kind, real or online.

Support

Questions, bug reports or privacy requests: oddscam26@gmail.com. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you use and, for card-reading problems, describe the lighting and the deck.

Tips for reading cards

  • Hold the cards still, close to the camera, in even light without glare.
  • Keep the top-left corner of each card (rank and suit) uncovered.
  • If your deck is misread, use the deck-teaching option in the scan menu, or pick the cards by hand.
  • Odds are estimates and the camera can misread cards. Check them before relying on them.

Camera access

If the camera is off, go to Settings › Odds Cam › Camera and switch it on. You can still pick cards by hand without the camera.
