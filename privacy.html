Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

In short: Odds Cam has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer collects no personal data. Camera images are processed on your device and are never saved or sent anywhere.

Who we are

Odds Cam is published by Prashant Baliga, based in the United Kingdom ("we", "us"). Contact: oddscam26@gmail.com.

What the app does with your camera

The app asks for camera access so it can read playing cards. Each video frame is analysed on your device, held in memory only as long as needed, and then discarded. Frames and photos are not saved, uploaded or shared, and the app does not use your photo library.

What is stored on your device

  • Settings such as whether the camera starts automatically, the selected game mode and display options.
  • Deck templates (optional). If you use the deck-teaching feature, the app stores small black-and-white patterns cut from the corners of cards you taught it, so it can recognise your deck. They are kept in the app's private storage on your device. Use "Forget all" in the app, or delete the app, to remove them.

This information stays on your device. We cannot see it.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, identifiers, usage data or diagnostics, and the app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software and makes no network requests of its own.

Apple

Apple may collect crash reports, analytics or purchase information if you have chosen to share them in your device settings or when you download the app. That is governed by Apple's privacy policy. We may see aggregated, anonymous figures (such as download counts or crash totals) through App Store Connect, which cannot identify you.

Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and we collect no personal information from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is normally nothing to access, correct, export or delete. If you believe we hold information about you, or you have any privacy question, contact us at the address above and we will respond within a reasonable time. Under UK and EU data protection law you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority; in the UK this is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Residents of US states with privacy laws have equivalent rights, and we do not sell or share personal information.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app handles data (for example by adding advertising or analytics), we will update this policy and the App Privacy details on the App Store before the change is released. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
